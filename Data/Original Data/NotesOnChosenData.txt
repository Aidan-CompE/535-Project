We chose ten of the participants because we believe it gives a good variety of data to feed to the model while keeping in mind limited storage and demanding script run times.

The choices avoid a known problem in the dataset. Participants 1-41 had inconsistent motion sampling rates due to a software issue so we avoided those. 

Each chosen participant has their motion and gaze recordings. There is some missing data but the main files we need are included for all of them.

Each of the choices have two recording sessions allowing us to train our classifier on one and use the other for the testing.
